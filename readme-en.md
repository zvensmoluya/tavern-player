# Tavern Player

> **Built for the Tavern ecosystem. Designed for play.**

Tavern Player is an Android-native roleplay client built for the ecosystem that grew around SillyTavern.

Character cards have become more than character cards. They carry lore, regex, presentation tricks, presets, and years of community experimentation.

We want to keep all of that power — without asking players to manage all of it.

## The idea

Most of the time, you should only need three things:

**Character** — what you want to experience.
**Preset** — how you want it written.
**Model** — who generates it.

Then chat.

The complicated parts can stay complicated underneath.

They just don't need to become your problem.

> **Complex characters. Simple product.**

SillyTavern built the ecosystem.

We're building the player.
